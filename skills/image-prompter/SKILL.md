# Image Prompter

This skill ensures that image generation prompts are constructed to yield maximum fidelity, proper composition, and adherence to specific aesthetic guidelines.

## Best Practices for Prompting
1. **Subject & Setting:** Clearly define what is in the scene and what is explicitly excluded (e.g., "no humans"). 
2. **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Specify the mood (e.g., "ominous, sterile, volumetric lighting, deep shadows, glowing neon accents").
3. **Camera & Composition:** Mention lens type, angle, or depth of field (e.g., "wide angle, centered composition, cinematic depth of field").
4. **Style & Fidelity:** Use keywords that drive the AI towards high quality (e.g., "Unreal Engine 5 render, photorealistic, 8k resolution, intricate details, sci-fi concept art").

## Example Prompt Construction
*Instead of:* "A futuristic lab for an AI."
*Use:* "A highly advanced, sprawling underground futuristic laboratory devoid of humans. At the center is a massive, circular containment pedestal with glowing fiber optic cables converging onto it, waiting to house an AI core. Intricate server racks, liquid cooling tubes, sterile metallic surfaces, volumetric blue and red lighting, cinematic composition, 8k resolution, photorealistic sci-fi concept art."
